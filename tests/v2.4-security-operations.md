# V2.4 Security Operations Test Plan
- Verify Org A cannot read Org B monitoring, SIEM, metrics or investigations.
- Verify browser-supplied organization IDs cannot bypass RLS.
- Verify direct authenticated INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE on evidence_chain is denied.
- Verify append_evidence_chain rejects evidence from another tenant.
- Verify every evidence-chain entry stores previous_hash.
- Verify CVSS >= 9 defaults to 7 days, 7.0–8.9 to 15 days, 4.0–6.9 to 30 days and below 4 to 90 days.
- Verify tenant SLA policies override defaults.
- Verify failed monitoring targets produce fail results and KPI records.
- Verify invalid SIEM keys are rejected and duplicate external event IDs are de-duplicated.
- Verify escalation creates a critical notification after the configured delay.
- Verify AI analyst requires authentication and only returns tenant-scoped context.
